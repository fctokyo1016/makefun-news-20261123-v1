# Monday 23 November 2026: the world in one day (41 stories)

An entry for the [make.fun bounty
`news-image-20261123`](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123),
which closes Tue, Nov 25, 2026, 09:00 JST. Run it with
`python3 main.py` and the front page lands at `/output/entry.png`.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the finished page: 1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MiB,
  drawn for Monday, 23 November 2026.
- `render.py` — the program that draws `entry.png` from scratch
  with Python and PIL. Rerun it and the same bytes come out again.
- `main.py` — the entrypoint: it checks the PNG magic bytes and
  the 1 MiB cap, then copies the page to `/output/entry.png`.
  No network, no randomness.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## The page

"THE WORLD IN ONE DAY" fits **41 stories** from Monday,
23 November 2026 on one newspaper page: a masthead carrying the
three biggest stories of the day, six headline numbers, a bar of
the seven regions, a UTC timeline, three illustrated leads,
twelve short cards, a dotted world map with numbered pins, 26
one-line briefs, and a date-check box naming what was left out
because it belongs to another day.

Each item is dated to 23 November 2026 and tagged with a source
and a certainty level: CONFIRMED means two or more wires
carried it, REPORTED means the day's wires carried it, CLAIM
means it is one side's account.

This is the v2 final version, rebuilt once Monday (UTC) had
closed. The leads are COP31 in Antalya ending with no cover
decision after a record 49 hours of overtime — with Monday's
reactions, from Guterres's "deep dismay" to Gore calling the
fossil-fuel veto "outrageous"; Belgium's national strike, with
404 of 429 Brussels Airport flights cancelled and 50,000 people
marching in Brussels; and Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as
CEO after President Trump's intervention, the shares jumping 31%
and adding about $950bn in a day while the Nasdaq gained 6.5%.
The reporting was checked against the wires and major outlets of
the day, among them Reuters, AP, AFP, CNN, Al Jazeera, RTBF,
Belga, Xinhua, SCMP, The Times, El País, GMA, Haaretz, KCNA,
ATP, Investopedia and USA Today.
